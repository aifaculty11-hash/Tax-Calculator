📖 Capital Gains & Special Rate — Quick Reference

Key rules for AY 2026-27 (FY 2025-26) as per Finance Act 2025

📉
STCG u/s 111A — 20%
Equity & equity MF held ≤ 12 months. Rate increased from 15% to 20% via Finance (No.2) Act 2024. STT must be paid on sale.
📈
LTCG u/s 112A — 12.5%
Equity & equity MF held > 12 months. ₹1,25,000 exemption per year. Rate increased from 10% to 12.5% w.e.f. 23 Jul 2024. Surcharge capped at 15%.
🏠
LTCG on Property — 12.5%
Immovable property held > 24 months. Indexation removed w.e.f. 23 Jul 2024. For property acquired before that date, option available for 20% with indexation.
🪙
Gold & Debt MF
Physical gold >24 months: 12.5% LTCG. Debt MF purchased after 1 Apr 2023: gains taxed at slab rates (no LTCG). Pre Apr 2023 debt MF: 12.5% LTCG.
₿
Crypto / VDA — 30%
All virtual digital assets taxed at flat 30% u/s 115BBH regardless of holding period. No deduction. No loss set-off. 1% TDS u/s 194S.
🎰
Lottery / Games — 30%
Winnings from lottery, horse racing, card games, reality shows & online gaming taxed at 30%. TDS deducted at source. No deduction allowed.
🔒
87A Rebate & Special Income
Rebate u/s 87A is NOT available on STCG (111A) and LTCG (112A). For other special income (VDA, lottery), rebate applies if normal income is within limits.
💵
Dividend — Surcharge Capped at 15%
Dividend from domestic companies & MFs is taxable at slab rates (DDT abolished FY 2020-21). Surcharge on dividend income is capped at 15% regardless of total income level (Finance Act). Relevant for Old Regime taxpayers with income > ₹2Cr. 10% TDS u/s 194 on dividend > ₹5,000 per company per year.
📊
Surcharge on CG
Surcharge on LTCG u/s 112A is capped at 15%. For LTCG u/s 112 (other assets), marginal surcharge rates apply. VDA / lottery attract full surcharge.
