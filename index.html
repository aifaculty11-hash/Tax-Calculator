Income Tax Act 2025 — Effective from 1 April 2026 (Tax Year 2026-27 onwards)
The Income Tax Act 2025 replaces the Income Tax Act 1961. No change in slab rates, deduction limits, or rebate amounts. Key changes: New "Tax Year" terminology replaces FY/AY duality · Section numbers renumbered throughout (80C → Sec 123, 87A → Sec 157, 115BAC → Sec 202) · Income Tax Rules 1962 replaced by Income Tax Rules 2026 · Revised return deadline extended to 31 March (fee applicable after 9 months) · Form 15G/15H merged into Form 121 · TAN not required for NRI property TDS.

📖 Capital Gains & Special Rate — Quick Reference

Key rules for Tax Year 2026-27 (FY 2026-27) as per Income Tax Act 2025

📉
STCG — 20%
Equity & equity MF held ≤ 12 months. Rate increased from 15% to 20% via Finance (No.2) Act 2024. STT must be paid on sale.
📈
LTCG — 12.5%
Equity & equity MF held > 12 months. ₹1,25,000 exemption per year. Rate increased from 10% to 12.5% w.e.f. 23 Jul 2024. Surcharge capped at 15%.
🏠
LTCG on Property — 12.5%
Immovable property held > 24 months. Indexation removed w.e.f. 23 Jul 2024. For property acquired before that date, option available for 20% with indexation.
🪙
Gold & Debt MF
Physical gold >24 months: 12.5% LTCG. Debt MF purchased after 1 Apr 2023: gains taxed at slab rates (no LTCG). Pre Apr 2023 debt MF: 12.5% LTCG.
₿
Crypto / VDA — 30%
All virtual digital assets taxed at flat 30% regardless of holding period. No deduction. No loss set-off. 1% TDS on transfer.
🎰
Lottery / Games — 30%
Winnings from lottery, horse racing, card games, reality shows & online gaming taxed at 30%. TDS deducted at source. No deduction allowed.
🔒
87A Rebate & Special Income
Rebate u/s 87A is NOT available on STCG (111A) and LTCG (112A). For other special income (VDA, lottery), rebate applies if normal income is within limits.
💵
Dividend — Surcharge Capped at 15%
Dividend from domestic companies & MFs is taxable at slab rates. Surcharge on dividend income is capped at 15% regardless of total income level. Relevant for Old Regime taxpayers with income > ₹2Cr. 10% TDS on dividend > ₹5,000 per company per year.
🏠
House Property Income — Key Rules
Self-Occupied: Annual Value = Nil. Interest max ₹2L in Old Regime; NIL in New Regime. Let-Out: 30% of NAV auto-deducted. Interest without limit. Old Regime: Loss set-off ≤ ₹2L/yr; balance c/f 8 yrs. New Regime: Let-out loss cannot be set off against other income.
